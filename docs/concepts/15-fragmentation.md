# Fragmentation

## Definition

Monetary fragmentation measures transition friction across ledgers, issuers, liquidity pools, settlement systems, identity domains, jurisdictions and access models.

## Purpose

Identify where monetary mobility friction is concentrated.

## Inputs

Weighted pairwise transition frictions.

## Outputs

Experimental OMST metric and concentration findings.

## State Assumptions

MFI is not an official systemic-risk metric.

## Examples

High friction between two ledgers due to finality mismatch and liquidity shortage.

## Non-Examples

Regulatory stress test certification.

## Failure Modes

Bad weights, unnormalised friction and missing context.

## Relationship To Other OMST Concepts

Fragmentation builds on relations, routing, mobility, liquidity and settlement context.
