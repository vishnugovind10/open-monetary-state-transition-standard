# Transition Evidence

## Definition

Transition evidence explains why OMST believes a transition occurred or can occur.

## Purpose

Separate declared, observed, derived and simulated support.

## Inputs

Issuer declarations, ledger observations, receipts, proofs, attestations, policy documents, regulatory sources, market observations, calculations, simulations and manual assertions.

## Outputs

Evidence records and evidence-chain steps.

## State Assumptions

Issuer-declared evidence is not automatically observed evidence.

## Examples

A synthetic ledger event plus a synthetic redemption policy.

## Non-Examples

Unattributed claims or copied private research.

## Failure Modes

Evidence poisoning, stale observations, broken provenance and unsupported confidence.

## Relationship To Other OMST Concepts

Evidence supports capabilities, profiles, transitions, events and evaluations.
