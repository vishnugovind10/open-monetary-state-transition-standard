# Liquidity

## Definition

Liquidity is decomposed into nominal, settlement-ready, immediately available, redeemable, convertible, venue, institutionally accessible and effective liquidity.

## Purpose

Distinguish money that exists from money usable for a specific settlement.

## Inputs

Liquidity profile, function, venue, institution and timestamp.

## Outputs

Component liquidity and effective liquidity.

## State Assumptions

Market cap, total supply and TVL are not sufficient liquidity evidence.

## Examples

EUR 1bn nominal supply but EUR 50m effective DvP liquidity.

## Non-Examples

Unqualified supply metrics.

## Failure Modes

Stale liquidity, inaccessible balance, venue incompatibility and redemption bottleneck.

## Relationship To Other OMST Concepts

Liquidity constrains transition cost, mobility, routing and evaluation.
