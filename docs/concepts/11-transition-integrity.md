# Transition Integrity

## Definition

Transition integrity evaluates whether required monetary properties were preserved, degraded, improved, incomparable or unknown.

## Purpose

Provide a multidimensional alternative to arbitrary money scores.

## Inputs

Transition, context, source profile, target profile and evidence.

## Outputs

Dimension-level integrity results and reasons.

## State Assumptions

The aggregate is not the canonical truth; dimensions remain visible.

## Examples

Finality preserved, liquidity degraded, access preserved.

## Non-Examples

`transition_integrity = 87.4312`.

## Failure Modes

Finality mismatch, liquidity insufficiency, missing evidence or inaccessible venue.

## Relationship To Other OMST Concepts

Integrity feeds transition evaluation, mobility and routing.
