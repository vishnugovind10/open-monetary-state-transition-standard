# Monetary Economics Context

M0/M1/M2 are monetary-economic classification concepts. OMST does not establish official monetary aggregates or determine regulatory classification.

OMST uses monetary hierarchy as conceptual context for functional representation, state representation and transition representation.

Traditional macroeconomic money velocity is distinct from OMST settlement velocity. OMST settlement velocity measures how intensively settlement-ready digital monetary balances support settlement activity over an observation window.
