# ECB Context

NON-NORMATIVE.

ECB materials describe Pontes as a Eurosystem DLT solution linking market DLT platforms and TARGET Services for central-bank-money settlement. Public ECB material also describes Appia as longer-term work on a blueprint for a European tokenised financial ecosystem, including interoperability, standards, cross-border connectivity, legal and regulatory foundations, and resilience.

OMST sits below this kind of architecture as a technical representation layer for monetary state, transition evidence and transition integrity. It is not a TARGET component and claims no affiliation with the ECB.

Sources: ECB Pontes, ECB Appia, ECB speech on Europe's tokenised financial ecosystem.
