# SDRB / ReserveFi Boundary

SDRB-type systems focus on reserve infrastructure, treasury, proof of reserves, governance, capital protection and stable layers.

OMST focuses on money profiles, money states, money transitions, transition evidence, transition integrity, settlement velocity, transition cost and monetary mobility.

The relationship is complementary rather than adversarial. A future OMST adapter may ingest reserve-evidence data, but OMST does not become the reserve system.
