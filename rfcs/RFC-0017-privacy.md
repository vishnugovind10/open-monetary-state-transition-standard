# RFC-0017: Privacy

Status: draft

## Abstract

Defines the future `PrivacyProfile` scope for public evidence, hashed identity, credential references, zero-knowledge attestations and encrypted evidence references.

## Motivation

Settlement evidence can be sensitive even when transition semantics are public.
